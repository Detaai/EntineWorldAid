Randeth Vision — Image & Live Camera Filter

Simulates Randeth perception: mostly grayscale with subtle green sensitivity, and options to force-merge foliage & Entian-like colors.

Tip: Upload an image or use your camera. Tweak sliders to control how strongly greens are flattened or boosted. "Force Merge Greens" maps all green-dominant pixels into a narrow brightness band (makes foliage and Entian tones indistinguishable).
